## ✦ 2. What to Tell the Client to Provide (Client Action Items)

To take the store live with real customer transactions, the client only needs to provide:
1. **Custom Domain**: Access to their domain registrar (e.g. GoDaddy / Namecheap) to point `labelsurbhee.com` to Shopify.
2. **Shopify Subscription**: A Shopify Basic account (~₹20 trial / ₹1,999/mo).
3. **Payment Gateway (UPI & Cards)**: A merchant account on **Razorpay** or **Cashfree** (requires GSTIN, PAN, and business bank account).
4. **Logistics Courier Partner**: A **Shiprocket** or **Delhivery** account for automated doorstep pickups from their Mumbai atelier.
5. **WhatsApp Business Number**: Their official atelier phone number to link the 1-click *"Chat with Bridal Concierge"* button.
