Three ways to find your parcel — pick whichever suits you.

1 · Your account

Log in → Orders shows every order with its live fulfilment status, from confirmed to delivered.

2 · Your shipping email

Every dispatch email carries a tracking link from our courier partner — it updates at each hub scan.

3 · WhatsApp

Send us your order number and a human replies with the latest status — usually within minutes, 11 am – 7 pm, Mon – Sat.

Ordered as a guest? No problem — use the shipping email link, or WhatsApp us your order number.
